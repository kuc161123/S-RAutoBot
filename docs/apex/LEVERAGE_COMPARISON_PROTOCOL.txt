5X VERSUS 10X PERPETUAL RESEARCH — 2026-10-05

Fixed before inspecting this experiment's outcomes. Offline research only.
User explicitly selected unchanged risk per trade. No live account, leverage,
Railway, Telegram, strategy configuration, orders or ledger changes.

Primary question
Compare 5x and 10x isolated-margin settings with the same cautious risk budget
(0.25% of current account equity before existing setup multipliers), unchanged
portfolio caps and identical Elliott Wave rules. Leverage must never multiply
quantity or profit. Both long and short opportunities are evaluated. This
differs from the preceding five-symbol long-only spot comparison.

Universe/data plan
Select 50 USDT perpetual symbols by median turnover over the seven completed
UTC days immediately before 2023-11-27. Require at least 120 completed daily
bars of prior hourly history. Freeze membership for this experiment, without
looking at future strategy performance. Record every eligibility exclusion.
Use the existing Bybit public historical caches, hash and validate their
chronology/OHLCV. Do not manufacture missing candles or splice exchange venues.
Require complete hourly entry/runoff coverage. If unavailable, document the
coverage problem and a pre-outcome amendment; never hide removed symbols or
label a cache-constrained universe as the exact historical exchange top 50.
This is a fixed historical liquidity proxy, not the live rotating universe:
historical order-book depth/spread and a complete historical listing universe
are not available. Delisted/renamed instruments and cache availability can
introduce selection bias. Future instrument metadata is not an eligibility
filter or a reconstruction of historical lot sizes/risk tiers.

Entry period: 2023-11-27 00:00 through 2025-11-26 00:00 UTC exclusive (730 days).
Runoff to 2026-05-25 00:00 UTC (180 further days); no new entries during runoff.
Each symbol retains its fixed contiguous warmup origin, with at least 120 days
before the common entry start. One shared 10,000 USDT account per scenario.

Rules/costs
Daily wave plans, closed 4H structure, 30-day watch, hourly closed quote and
next-hour-open IOC proxy with the existing 5bp cap. Existing stops, partial
TP1, TP2, daily invalidation, structural trailing and time exits. Stops win
OHLC ties and gap through stops uses the worse open. No credited entry-bar
targets. No forced end exits; open trades are not wins/losses.
Current sizing/cost-aware R:R, daily/weekly/drawdown halts and exposure caps.
Same-time candidates sorted by symbol then plan ID. Rejected unconsumed IDs
may retry; accepted IDs cannot duplicate. Each scenario independently sizes
from its own chronological shared account, including pending reservations.
Synthetic instrument tick/lot 0.0001 and 5 USDT minimum notional, as in the
prior research; not a historical reconstruction of each exchange filter.
Fees 0.055% per fill, 0.01% spread and 0.03% adverse slippage each side. These
are stated scenarios, not observed fills or the authenticated account tier.
Historical funding applied at settlement, remaining ENTRY notional basis,
strict entry/exit ties excluded, with only already-settled rates used by risk
decisions. Rate intervals may change: infer normalization from past intervals
only, and report coverage/interval validation limitations explicitly.

Margin/liquidation distinction
Mirror the runtime's conservative entry rule exactly:
  entry * (1 / leverage - maintenance_rate - 0.002) >= 2 * stop_distance.
The 0.002 reserve and twice-stop distance are existing bot rules. Historical
exchange maintenance tiers and mark-price candles are not in the cache.
Use an explicit assumed maintenance rate of 1% for the primary pair; also run
both leverages at 0.5% and 2.5% as predeclared sensitivity scenarios, not tuned
parameters. Never present assumed maintenance rates as actual symbol tiers.
Reserve open entry notional / leverage plus an estimated closing fee, pending
order margin plus fees, and report available cash conservatively. Unrealized
profits do not finance new isolated positions. Unrealized losses reduce equity
and therefore risk sizing; they are covered by the allocated isolated margin
and are not deducted a second time from free wallet cash. Funding/fees affect
equity and cash before sizing; any free-cash shortfall must be reported.
Track stop distance and margin buffer, plus hourly adverse-range breaches of
the simplified isolated threshold. Such breaches are stress flags, not proven
liquidations: mark-price history and intrahour ordering are unavailable. Do
not certify zero liquidations from last-traded hourly bars. If flags occur,
headline returns remain stop-model estimates and must be labeled accordingly.

Coverage resolution before results
All 50 selected histories have complete hourly entry/runoff coverage; none
was removed or replaced after selection. The 158 eligible cached histories
include available delisted histories, but are not a complete exchange census.
Forty-four selected symbols have matching funding timestamps/rates in both
local funding caches throughout the window; six have one funding cache only.
BLUR, INJ, GMT, ZRX and LUNA2 contain isolated 5/6/7-hour transition intervals
in runoff that match both caches. Allow only those explicitly audited UTC
timestamps as exceptions, preserve observed rates, and normalize from elapsed
past settlement time. Other intervals must be 1/2/4/8 hours. This agreement is
local consistency evidence, not an independent exchange completeness proof.
Open/pending closing-fee reserves use entry*(1+1/leverage)*fee as a conservative
convention for both directions. Reserved fees are not expenses until a fill.
Monitored short orders reserve margin at the upper entry-zone boundary because
a favorable fill at a higher price can require more collateral. This does not
increase quantity; risk sizing remains at the original conservative entry cap.
