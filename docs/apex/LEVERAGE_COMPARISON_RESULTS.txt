5X VERSUS 10X PERPETUALS — COMPLETED 2026-10-05

Decision
Keep exchange execution disabled. Neither setting demonstrated a profitable
edge in this experiment. Do not promote 10x because it lost less: the tighter
liquidation-distance gate removed most candidates, and every filled 10x trade
lost. More leverage is not a remedy for signal quality or timing.

Why the leverage settings differ
The requested risk budget is unchanged: quantity is calculated from equity and
entry-to-stop risk, never multiplied by leverage. Leverage changes collateral
and the existing conservative liquidation-distance guard. With the primary
1% maintenance assumption, that rule accepts a stop at most 9.4% from entry at
5x, versus 4.4% at 10x. It rejected 194 unique opportunities at least once in
the 5x run and 285 in the 10x run. These are retry-aware, nonexclusive counts;
a previously rejected opportunity can later qualify.

No scenario exhausted free margin. The material difference therefore came
from the guard's eligibility/timing effects and subsequent portfolio paths,
not extra buying power. Seven filled opportunity IDs overlap between the
primary arms; 30 fill only at 5x and one only at 10x. This is not a paired
identical-fill experiment. Once histories diverge, equity-based sizing and
available position/bucket slots can also differ.

What was tested
- Entry window: 2023-11-27 to 2025-11-26 UTC exclusive (730 days).
  No-entry runoff: another 180 days, through 2026-05-25 UTC.
- 50 symbols ranked by median turnover over the seven completed days before
  entry start, among 158 eligible cached histories with >=120 prior daily bars.
  All 50 have complete entry/runoff hourly coverage; none was replaced after
  selection. This is a cache-constrained historical liquidity proxy, not the
  exact historical exchange census or today's dynamically rotating universe.
- The offline monitored-zone variant: daily wave structure, closed 4H evidence,
  up to 30 days of watching, then next-hour-open IOC proxy after an eligible
  in-zone closed quote. Existing stops, partial targets, invalidation and
  structure management; conservative OHLC ties and gap exits.
- One common account per arm with current portfolio/risk caps. No hindsight
  redistribution into only profitable symbols or directions.
- 0.055% fee per fill, 0.01% spread, 0.03% adverse slippage each side. Funding
  uses historical settlements on remaining ENTRY notional, an explicit
  estimate rather than historical mark notional. Synthetic exchange lot/tick
  rules are disclosed in the protocol; no actual account fee tier was queried.
- No historical AI/macro veto or live order-book/tick execution is modeled.
  These periods were previously studied and are not untouched out-of-sample.

Relation to the preceding positive perpetual result
The earlier five-symbol test was long-only and fully collateralized, without
this isolated-margin eligibility guard. This test uses 50 symbols, both sides,
and the guard. The earlier small gain cannot be generalized to this system or
attributed to leverage. Neither result establishes future profitability.

Recommended next step
Continue cloud shadow collection, including capacity-only independent observer
outcomes, while reviewing wave timing and trade quality. A separate predefined
1x/2x or stop-distance-derived leverage test could quantify how much the guard
discards, but no such result is claimed here. Do not widen live stops, relax
loss circuits or increase risk to compensate for this negative evidence.
Production still uses confirmed daily/4H signals; monitored-zone entries are
offline research only. No 10x live configuration or exchange leverage was set.
