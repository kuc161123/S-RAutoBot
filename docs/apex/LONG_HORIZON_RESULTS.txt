What this means

Daily-wave trades do need multi-week evaluation. The monitored approach improved
entry frequency, and both directions were exercised, but the aggregate was near
break-even before funding and slightly negative afterward. Results depend heavily
on BNB; short entries were weak in this sample. Investigate entry quality, short
setups and stop behavior in separate prospective tests. Do not cherry-pick BNB
or disable shorts based only on this same small sample. More symbols can provide
more observations; breadth alone does not establish an edge.

Funding and other limits

All five cached rate histories contain exactly the expected 2,730 eight-hour
observations in this span. Estimates use each recorded rate times remaining
ENTRY notional, with partial exits accounted for; actual settlement mark prices
are unavailable. Entry/exit timestamp ties are excluded. Rates are an after-run
estimate and do not retroactively change sizing decisions or claim exchange
accounting completeness. The apparent $5.43 price-and-fee gain is too small to
support live activation even before these execution uncertainties.

The source has a missing hour on May 25, 2026. This experiment ends before it;
no missing candles are interpolated. Prices are hourly and use the conservative
next-hour execution model from MONITORED_ZONE_PROTOCOL.txt. No AI/macro veto,
full portfolio loss-circuit path, actual spreads/depth or point-in-time rotating
universe was backtested. The fixed five are selected surviving symbols. Historical
results are conditional on this universe, history origin and these assumptions.

Reproducibility

LONG_HORIZON_PROTOCOL.txt was saved before the runs. Full evidence is under
long_horizon_evidence_20261005/: manifest.json, report.json, ten compressed raw
replays, analysis.json and trades.csv. Report checks verify code/source hashes,
full requested coverage, final replay timestamps and no fills past entry cutoff.
The management cache was checked against uncached open-to-close trajectories,
both directions, future perturbations, gaps, and bounded real-data paths.

The separate dynamic universe feature targets 50 eligible symbols, reviews every
six hours, and uses sustained turnover plus spread/depth checks. Normal rotation
requires a 50% score advantage at two successive observations, with up to five
healthy replacements per UTC day. Existing positions keep normal management.
See OPERATIONS.txt and /universe for the full policy. Local implementation and
offline validation do not constitute deployment; today's actual top 50 cannot
be verified from this machine because the unsigned Bybit request returns 403.
