MONITORED ZONE EXPERIMENT — PREDEFINED RULES
2026-10-05

Question: can an unexpired, structurally valid daily-wave plan wait for a fresh
zone arrival and enter with acceptable current R:R, without a resting order?
This protocol is recorded before examining any results from the new entry arm.
It is an offline experiment only. Live release and runtime settings stay intact.

Paired universe: BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT, BNBUSDT, LINKUSDT, Tier 1.
Primary window: 2026-02-24 00:00 UTC to 2026-05-25 00:00 UTC (same prior test).
Later validation: 90 days ending 2026-09-18 00:00 UTC. The source has a missing
hour on May 25, so validation uses the later contiguous segment's fresh origin;
no gap filling or carry-over positions/plans across that gap. Warmup is reported.

Arms: existing confirmation; existing 48-hour midpoint resting proposal;
monitored zone with 48-hour lifetime (duration control); monitored zone with
30-day lifetime (primary, matching the existing engine's maximum plan age).
No entry/stop/target/expiry parameter search and no changing these choices after
seeing results. Each arm starts independently flat with 10000 per symbol.

Rules common to monitored arms:
- Same causal frozen daily wave plans, anchors, zones, structural invalidation,
  hard stops, two targets, risk sizing, costs and R:R hurdles as the other arms.
- No 4H reversal confirmation is required or invented. The independent entry
  evidence is a current observed quote inside the frozen zone.
- Assess hourly with fully closed history only. Historical close is a quote
  proxy: add half the assumed 0.01% spread for buys, subtract it for sells.
- Set the worst acceptable IOC entry 0.05% beyond that quote, adverse to the
  trader. Both quote and rounded cap must remain in the strict zone. Risk is
  assessed at this worst acceptable price, including the unchanged cost budget.
- Simulate execution once at the next fully evidenced hour's OPEN, with the same
  spread proxy and an additional 0.03% adverse entry slippage. If this price is
  outside the cap/zone or beyond original invalidation, cancel immediately.
  A later low/high touching the cap never fills this immediate order. No retro-
  active entry on the hourly bar used to make the decision. One accepted order
  attempt per plan, as in the existing comparison; unfilled attempts are reported.
- Closed hourly hard-stop/target passage, applicable intrabar structural breach,
  daily-close invalidation, or age cancels the watch permanently. Price outside
  the zone or temporarily poor R:R means keep waiting until a terminal event.
- Conflicting valid long/short plans block entry. No moving old wave anchors or
  widening invalidation to rescue a failed plan. New evidence creates a new plan.
- Entry-bar stop takes precedence; no entry-bar target credit. Existing partial
  T1, T2, structural and time management continue. Open end-of-window positions
  stay open and are reported; no fabricated final exits.

Report unique plans, zone-ready candidates, accepted orders, fills, closed/open,
wins/losses/breakeven, WR denominator, before-funding PnL, and rejection/cancel
reasons. Separate repeated observations from unique setups. Record source hashes,
coverage, complete flags, code policy and assumptions. Any incomplete run is a
labeled diagnostic, never a complete zero-trade result.

Limitations: hourly data cannot establish tick fills, actual spread, queue depth,
partial IOC fills, outages or precise intrahour ordering. Fees and slippage are
assumed. No AI, macro filter or live account; independent symbol books do not
validate aggregate portfolio risk or loss circuit paths. Funding is not assumed
proven zero; reported realized PnL is BEFORE funding. The later window is a
chronological validation, not a statistically untouched dataset guarantee.

Acceptance: the test can demonstrate coherent behavior and expose failure modes.
Few trades or one period cannot demonstrate a reliable profitable edge. Results
inform a recommendation, not authorization to submit exchange orders or deploy.
