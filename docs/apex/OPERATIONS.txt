APEX CLOUD BOT — IMPLEMENTATION AND OPERATIONS
2026-10-05

Current state

The new implementation is in apex_bot/. It defaults to SHADOW: public market
data, hypothetical trades, AI review and Telegram reporting; no exchange orders.
The existing Apex Drive/Sheet ledger is read-only and is not the bot's database.
Existing strategy/research files are preserved. legacy_main.py is the original
main.py entrypoint; main.py now selects Apex unless AUTOBOT_RUNTIME=legacy.
Dockerfile.apex intentionally contains only the new runtime.

This is a conservative numerical implementation of a subset of the Apex method,
not a reproduction of every discretionary analyst decision. ENGINE_RULES.txt
lists each formula, engineering interpretation and unsupported case. Weekly
degree selection, complex corrections/diagonals, discretionary recounts, resting
ladders and the complete TradingView/Claude workflow are not automated here.
Two blind model reviews critique the same candidate; they are not independent
full-market Elliott counts. Agreement is not a win probability.

Pipeline

1. Bybit public endpoints provide instrument rules, UTC daily/4H closed candles,
   quotes, spread and funding rates. Only confirmed pivots and later confirmed
   structure can create a candidate. Both long and short mirrors are supported.
2. PostgreSQL stores a fixed market-history origin, opportunities, execution
   intents, outcomes, settings, a single-worker lease and a durable Telegram
   outbox. A changed historic candle is rejected for review, not silently used
   to rewrite signals. Redis is an optional disposable public-data cache.
3. The rules-only shadow book simulates eligible candidates. OpenAI's Responses
   API receives a frozen, bounded evidence packet twice in independent calls.
   Both valid approvals are required for the AI-selected book/live gate. Model
   output cannot alter entry, stop, targets, quantities or hard risk limits.
4. Optional Drive service-account reads import explicitly allowlisted Docs and
   selected Settings/Counts/Levels/CCR Sheet ranges. Revisions/hashes are stored
   separately. Fresh, bounded symbol-related extracts may inform AI critique;
   they cannot execute instructions or change numeric trading policy. No writes
   to Drive, Docs or Sheets are implemented. A connected desktop Drive login
   does not provision cloud credentials automatically.
5. A native context service uses BTC daily structure and official event calendars.
   Its market proxy is explicitly different from Apex's full macro/DXY process.
   Missing calendar/context evidence blocks new exchange entries. Optional
   external feed and OpenAI web research are advisory evidence, not policy
   authority. Public research is limited to primary-source domains.
6. The independent risk gate checks data freshness, spread/funding, reward/risk,
   precision, quantity/notional constraints, portfolio heat and loss limits.
   A live entry also needs fresh reconciliation, isolated one-way settings,
   liquidation-distance allowance and healthy Telegram delivery.
7. Stable order IDs and durable intents precede any exchange request. Ambiguous
   submissions are queried, not blindly resent. ACK is not a fill. Ownership,
   execution records and current size must reconcile before management. Unknown
   or external positions/orders block entries; the bot does not adopt them.
8. Exchange-side stops remain primary protection. Confirmed partial target fills
   govern break-even changes; structure management and recovery use closed bars.
   Exits use exchange trigger/fill evidence, not a guessed label based on price.
   Missing accounting/funding evidence is visible and blocks further execution.
9. Telegram receives state transitions, candidates, approvals/rejections/waits,
   fills/exits, reference changes and operational faults through the outbox.
   Repeated unchanged scans do not send another copy of every decision. Delivery
   is at least once: a timeout after Telegram accepted a message can duplicate it.

Risk controls

Profiles (percentage points of sizing equity, before reductions):
  ultra_cautious 0.10%; cautious 0.25%; balanced 0.50%; aggressive 0.75%; extreme 1%.
Custom risk through Telegram is 0.05%–1.00%. Tier/setup/context/drawdown reductions
can lower the result. "Extreme" does not disable safety or change wave rules.
Hard maxima: 4% open risk, 2% per bucket, six positions/two per bucket,
25% single-position notional, 100% gross notional, verified isolated leverage <=5.
Daily loss gate 2%, weekly 4%, halve risk at 8% strategy drawdown, stop at 12%.
UTC accounting; open losses are included conservatively even if carried from
an earlier day. Deposits/withdrawals are not strategy profits. Portfolio risk
caps and risk budgets do not guarantee a maximum realized loss during a gap.
No automatic live promotion or runtime self-modification is implemented.
The current source-controlled release decision in apex_bot/release.py also
blocks LIVE mode. Credentials, environment flags, Telegram and AI approval
cannot override this acceptance result. Shadow and testnet remain available.

Telegram

Both TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID and the sender's positive numeric TELEGRAM_ALLOWED_USER_IDS
must match, including read commands and buttons. @AutotradingBot222_bot is the
bot's username, not the owner's authorization ID. Comma-separated numeric IDs
allow multiple controllers. Unknown users receive no private account details.
Use /help for the registered menu. Dashboard, opportunities/why, waves, positions,
performance, research, system/telemetry, profile/risk, pause and resume have
handlers. Risk changes show old/new settings and require an expiring actor-bound
confirmation. Cancellation is persisted. /start opens the dashboard, not trading.
Pause stops new entries; protective management continues. It is not liquidation.
Mode/live deployment authorizations are not editable through Telegram buttons.

Performance interpretation

Rules-only baseline and AI-selected shadow books are independent hypothetical
portfolios. They show pending/open/closed/expired, wins/losses/breakeven, win rate,
estimated net and outstanding funding/data evidence. A signal is not an entry.
Shadow entries require a subsequent fully observed candle; entry-bar targets
are not awarded; existing stops win ambiguous same-bar ties. Costs assume 6bps
per fill and 3bps exit slippage. Historical funding rates use remaining entry
notional, not the unknown historical settlement mark, so the amount is estimated.
AI selection plus decision latency, sizing and book exposure can change outcomes;
the comparison reports these confounds and is not causal evidence of AI uplift.
Exchange results are separate and need actual transaction/accounting evidence.
No closed trades means no measured win rate, not a fabricated 0% success rate.

Replace placeholders privately in Railway; do not put credentials in Git/chat.
Follow-up configuration: OPENAI_MODEL is now staged as the documented fixed
snapshot gpt-5.4-mini-2026-03-17, and the user's supplied numeric Telegram ID
has been staged in TELEGRAM_ALLOWED_USER_IDS. The user reports entering the

no Apply/Deploy was performed. OPENAI_MODEL remains configurable.
The API key requires API billing/model access; a ChatGPT desktop subscription
does not substitute for an API key. OPENAI_MODEL must support Responses structured
outputs and web_search for the advisory research feature. Verify model access
with a bounded staging smoke test before production; no real calls were made here.

Review apex.env.example. Use RAILWAY_DOCKERFILE_PATH=Dockerfile.apex, one replica,
always-on compute, healthcheck /healthz on PORT, and persistent DATABASE_URL.
Set APEX_MODE=shadow, APEX_LIVE_ENABLED=false, APEX_MIGRATION_VERIFIED=false.
/healthz is process liveness for deployment overlap; /readyz additionally requires
current leadership and a successful recent market scan. A healthy liveness
response alone does not mean the bot is ready to trade.
Liveness stays unsuccessful until database/ledger compatibility, public instrument
access and configured Telegram identity pass read-only startup checks.
Do not use deprecated Nixpacks/legacy railway.json as the new build definition.

Before replacing a running bot, reconcile its positions and pending orders and
ensure they have an explicit management owner. The new bot does not reconstruct
old strategy identity/stop policy from balances. Stop the old Telegram poller
before using the same token in Apex. Its old lease does not coordinate with the
new apex_lease. Prefer a separate staging service, new database and bot token.
Do not enable live while validation/credentials/migration checks are incomplete.

Recommended release sequence:
  A. Run isolated tests and Docker/Postgres CI against this exact revision.
  B. Deploy shadow to staging with real public-data, calendar, Telegram and
     bounded AI connectivity checks. Test pause, risk preview/cancel/confirm,
     restart/lease handover, missing Redis and missing DB behavior.
  C. Use a separate TESTNET database, API keys and APEX_MODE=testnet to verify
     minimum-size order/fill, stop, partial target, timeout and restart behavior.
     Never use a production exchange ledger for testnet or vice versa.
  D. Require prospective results after costs, representative two-sided setups,
     operational observations and resolved migration evidence before live.
     Setting APEX_LIVE_ENABLED/APEX_MIGRATION_VERIFIED=true is an operator
     attestation, not a substitute for these checks or a profit guarantee.
     A reviewed source release must also explicitly clear the current strategy
     validation block; this implementation has not earned that clearance.

fallback. The CI workflow has a disposable Postgres integration test and Docker
build. TEST_APEX_DATABASE_URL is for that disposable test DB only; DATABASE_URL
is never read by those tests.

Operational limits and unresolved release evidence

The default universe now targets 50 eligible liquid USDT perpetuals. /universe
and the dashboard show persisted membership, eligible entry count, freshness,
replacement policy and draining positions. This does not grant more portfolio
risk or turn research entry modes into live trading. APEX_UNIVERSE_MODE=static
explicitly selects the APEX_SYMBOLS list; dynamic mode does not fall back to it.

Dynamic selection uses fresh paginated instrument metadata, all linear tickers,
and displayed book depth. Defaults: trading USDT perpetual, age >=30 days, no
pre-listing/stablecoin base/xstocks, 24h turnover >=20m USDT, spread <=10bps and
at least 25k USDT on EACH side within 25bps of mid. Depth checks cover the top
150 by sustained turnover after status/age/spread screening plus existing
qualified members, at four concurrent requests.
Book failure blocks entries for that member; a whole snapshot failure preserves
the prior snapshot and its age. Fewer than 50 qualifying symbols is shown as
underfilled rather than weakening the requirements to reach an arbitrary count.

Review every six hours, with failed attempts retried by a supervised five-minute
loop. Rank median 24h turnover from up to 28 observations over seven days; startup
uses available history and labels it explicitly. A healthy member is replaced
only if a challenger has >=1.5x its score at two successive six-hour observations,
with at most five such replacements per UTC day. Hard eligibility failures can
remove a symbol immediately at a review; eligible replacements fill those holes.
Selection, observations and replacement budgets survive restart in Postgres.
No fresh selection (or age >=24h) means no new entries. Current quotes and all
existing risk gates are still checked for each proposed entry.
Changing the target or liquidity thresholds requires matching revalidation;
an older snapshot cannot authorize entries under newly changed settings.
Explicit target reductions keep the highest-ranked members and preserve their
history, while excess members drain through normal position management.

Retired symbols with open positions continue structure, stops, targets, funding
and exchange reconciliation. Removal cancels unfilled entry remainders and does
not liquidate an existing position. Shadow recovery still resolves any fills
evidenced before retirement, rather than rewriting them as never entered.
Each execution intent saves its instrument rules. If a contract disappears from
fresh Trading metadata, reconciliation can use that saved metadata solely for
its owned position and pending remainder. It cannot restore entry permission.
Unknown/corrupt historical metadata remains a visible reconciliation blocker.
The initial dynamic selection starts with the supervised heartbeat, so a slow
book fetch does not starve the single-worker lease. Bybit HTTP 403 from this
local host prevents verifying today's actual membership here; Railway must
produce a fresh selection before any entries. No deployment was made locally.

Core state is transactionally stored as JSON with append-only events;
long-term retention/archival throughput must be measured before scaling.
Public calendar HTML/ICS formats can change; parsing failures visibly block new
entries. Freshness and startup checks are mandatory, not a calendar guarantee.
Local offline tests cannot certify Railway networking, real exchange behavior,
live Telegram permissions, actual AI model access or profitable strategy results.
Historical acceptance diagnostics are separate JSON files and include assumptions.
The initial 90-day BTC/ETH/SOL replay produced no eligible trades; do not treat
passing software tests as proof of a deployable trading edge.
The corrected v1.1 sequential 365-day audit also produced zero eligible trades
(6,570 checkpoints; 55 equality checks of offline memoization). The artifacts
record assumptions and warmup sensitivity. The separate slow, pre-fix parent
year-long replay was interrupted and is not counted as a completed test.
Those files preserve v1.1 results. The v1.2 timing/zone follow-up is documented
in TIMING_AND_ZONE_COMPARISON.txt and TIMING_ZONE_REPLAY.json. Its completed
five-symbol 90-day comparison produced zero approved confirmation orders and
three approved resting research orders, all expired unfilled. No profitability
inference is available. Resting candidates are offline-only, and exchange
submission explicitly rejects them; they are not enabled by deployment.
The subsequent monitored-zone experiment is documented in MONITORED_ZONE_RESULTS.txt.
It waits up to 30 days with fresh in-zone quote/R:R checks and models immediate
execution only in offline replay (--entry-style monitored_zone). It is NOT wired
to the runtime scan or Telegram activation. Six long fills across two windows,
only two closed outcomes and unknown funding are insufficient release evidence.
Exchange guards reject both monitored-zone and resting research candidates.
The subsequent 730-day entry plus 180-day runoff experiment has its own fixed
LONG_HORIZON_PROTOCOL.txt and long_horizon_evidence_20261005 artifacts. It uses
the original five-symbol paired universe, not a hindsight reconstruction of
historical top-50 membership. See LONG_HORIZON_RESULTS.txt for its results and
funding assumptions. Existing shorter reports are retained unchanged.
The unsigned public probe from this machine found Bybit HTTP 403 and BLS access
denied; the official Federal Reserve calendar parsed eight meetings. These are
host-specific results. Railway public-data/calendar connectivity remains a
release prerequisite, not an assumed success.
