SPOT VERSUS LONG-ONLY PERPETUAL RESEARCH
Fixed 2026-10-05 before running or inspecting this experiment's outcomes.

Question: Does cash-funded spot improve the existing monitored Elliott zone
strategy compared with long-only, fully collateralized perpetual exposure?
This is offline research, not permission to submit orders or deploy a switch.

Primary design
- BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT, BNBUSDT, LINKUSDT, fixed before results.
- Matching official Binance spot/perpetual hourly archives if complete and
  available. Validate archive SHA256 and candle chronology; do not manufacture
  hourly data from daily candles. Bybit's public API currently returns 403.
- Common history origin 2022-01-01 UTC. Entry period 2023-11-27 through
  2025-11-25 inclusive (730 days). No new decisions/fills from 2025-11-26.
  Follow existing holdings until 2026-05-25 00:00 UTC (180 additional days).
- Gaps/incomplete history fail validation. Any necessary coverage amendment
  must be recorded before replay, never chosen based on profit.
- Each arm starts with ONE shared 10,000 USDT account across all five symbols.
  No borrowing, external deposits, interest on idle cash, or short trades.
  Perpetual reserves full entry principal (1x) plus fees, for matched capital.
- Same frozen monitored-zone engine: daily wave plans, 4H structure, 30-day
  watch, last closed hourly quote, next-hour open IOC proxy with 5bp cap.
  Same invalidation, T1/T2 splitting, structural trail and timeout rules.
  Opposite bearish wave conflicts still veto longs; no removal of structure.
- Same cautious risk profile (0.25% before setup multipliers) and portfolio
  caps. Marked account equity funds sizing; pending orders reserve cash.
  Use original 2% daily, 4% weekly and 12% drawdown entry halts, plus the
  original risk reduction at 8% drawdown. Existing positions remain managed.
  Day/week reference is equity at current closed quotes on the first scan of
  UTC day/ISO week, prior to management. Global peak uses hourly marked equity.
- Same-time candidates are ordered alphabetically by symbol, then plan ID.
  No ranking using future outcome. Rejected unconsumed candidates may retry.
- Fees represent the published Bybit base VIP0 scenario: spot 0.10% per fill,
  perpetual 0.055% per fill; not the user's authenticated account fee tier or
  a reconstruction of historical fee schedules. Quote spread 0.01%, adverse
  entry and exit slippage 0.03% each. Actual spreads/depth are not available.
- Spot buy fees withheld in base: sizing represents NET owned/sellable units;
  cash cost is net quantity / (1 - fee) times fill price. Exit fees in USDT.
  Synthetic step/tick 0.0001, min notional 5; exchange-specific historic lot,
  gross buy rounding and dust remain approximations.
- Perpetual settled funding charged/credited chronologically to available
  cash and equity, using remaining ENTRY notional as an explicit estimate
  because historical settlement mark prices are unavailable. Strict entry/
  exit timestamp ties excluded; partial exits at settlement reduce quantity.
  Risk decisions use only the most recent already-settled funding rate.
  Spot funding is zero. Rates must cover the expected settlement schedule.
  Binance settlement timestamps can be milliseconds after their scheduled
  eight-hour boundary. Preserve those timestamps: allow at most 60 seconds
  offset for schedule validation, and apply each at the first subsequent
  hourly scan, never round it backwards into an earlier decision.
- Cost-aware R:R and sizing use entry fee equivalent + exit fee + 6bp
  roundtrip slippage, plus spread and latest known adverse funding. No
  post-result threshold, zone, stop or target optimization.

Report
Closed W/L/WR, open trades, realized net profit, marked equity, estimated
liquidation equity, hourly marked max drawdown, fees, estimated funding,
holding time, cash utilization, cap/cash rejections, per-symbol results and
calendar-year changes. No forced end exits and no open trade classified as
win/loss. Include equal-weight spot buy-and-hold as descriptive context only
(different continuous exposure/risk), cash baseline zero. Save full trades,
daily equity, code/source/protocol hashes and validation evidence.

Interpretation
Five fixed surviving coins are NOT the requested rotating top-50 universe.
Archive-venue results with a Bybit fee scenario are not Bybit fills. This
period overlaps prior research and is not untouched out-of-sample validation.
Neither a profitable small sample nor lower financing costs establishes an
edge. Report all arms, failures and unfinished trades. No production code,
Railway, Telegram, live account, API keys or external ledger changes.
No historical AI/macro veto, intrahour order book, partial entry fills or
exchange liquidation path is simulated. Perpetual mark-to-market uses candle
prices, not exchange mark prices. The fully collateralized comparison does
not establish the safety or returns of a leveraged perpetual account.
