APEX TIMING AND ZONE-ENTRY FOLLOW-UP
2026-10-05

Conclusion

Resting buys in valid bullish zones and resting sells in bearish zones are
coherent entry styles. They improve the proposed entry price relative to a
late confirmation, but remove the evidence that price has actually reversed.
Elliott targets are projections, not guaranteed exit prices. The comparison
does not justify live activation or relaxing the stop/R:R requirements.

Corrections made

1. Confirmed pivots retain opposite extremes observed during confirmation lag.
   The old reset could discard an intervening low/high and invent an anchor.
2. Trigger comparison evidence can predate the daily anchor; the latest swing
   must belong to the applicable correction and be known before the trigger.
3. A failed/expired alternate retest retires that attempt. A structurally valid
   deep-add plan can wait for a fresh ordinary trigger. An out-of-zone initial
   trigger is rejected without automatically retiring the entire setup.
4. The replay retries unconsumed eligible candidates until expiry, separates
   plans/orders/fills/closed trades, validates missing bars and reports partial
   runs honestly. No incomplete run is used as a completed benchmark.
5. Telegram /why now shows numerical net R:R, the required ratios, rounded
   target split when different, and a hypothetical price bound for passing
   nominal R:R. That bound is diagnostic, never an order authorization.

What was not changed

Hard stops, Elliott target arithmetic, fees/cost allowance, risk percentage,
position/notional/heat limits and required R:R remain intact. Daily Type 2
breakout-based plan creation remains; arming from the first closed 4H break
would need a separately specified causal endpoint freeze and comparison.

Resting experiment

Single fixed midpoint limit per Tier 1 plan, original structural stop,
50% at T1 then remaining quantity at T2/management exit, 48-hour expiry.
This is a simplified research alternative to Apex's three-order resting
ladder, not the full discretionary Apex process. Prices and targets freeze
using only information available at plan creation. No later data moves the
entry to manufacture a fill. A future complete candle is required to fill.
Stop-first handling applies to ambiguous candles; entry-bar targets are not
awarded. An unfilled order is cancelled if T1 passes first.

This experiment runs only when explicitly selected in the offline replay.
It is not enabled in the deployed bot or the new runtime's automatic scan.
Both live and testnet exchange submission reject resting research candidates.

Limitations and next research decision

Hourly execution data, synthetic instrument precision, assumed 0.01% spread
and zero funding for sizing; independent symbol books, no AI/macro selection
or portfolio loss-path validation. Historical funding is not certified. The
new replay uses the full contiguous source origin, so compare versions using
that same origin; do not attribute differences from older 500-bar warmups
solely to engine changes. Complete paired captures use the same window and
unchanged risk gates. Later touches after expiry are not fills.

The useful next experiment is causal earlier plan availability and/or an
explicitly longer, structurally monitored order lifetime for daily-wave setups.
Define that policy before testing it, preserve the 48-hour comparison, and
report both outcomes rather than selecting the best expiry after the fact.
A multi-order ladder also needs risk checks for partial fills, since only its
worst-priced leg may fill; a favorable theoretical full-ladder average is not
proof that a partially filled position has acceptable R:R.
