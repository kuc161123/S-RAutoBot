1. Confirmed swing evidence may predate the final daily anchor

SOURCE: 7.2, line 450: price enters the zone, then a 4H candle "closes above
the high of the most recent 4H lower-high", defined as "the last swing high
made while price was falling into the zone". Line 454 mirrors this for shorts:
the latest higher-low made while price was rising into the sell zone.

There is no condition that this swing, or its comparison predecessor, must
occur after W2/W5/B or after a software plan is created. ENGINE_RULES lines
131-136 add that restriction. Two comparison highs establish ONE lower-high;
the charter does not demand two successive lower-high formations.

MINIMAL CORRECTION: remove the blanket occurrence-time cutoff at the final
daily anchor for the comparison evidence. Keep confirmed pivots only, known
before the trigger bar opens, the latest relevant swing/predecessor comparison,
zone arrival, a NEW eligible closed-4H trigger and existing entry-price limits.
The predecessor may predate the anchor. The trigger swing may also predate it
if it belongs to the actual corrective approach into this entry zone.

LIMIT: this does not authorize any old pre-W2 swing. An old W2 correction is
not automatically the later deep-add correction after a rally through W1.
The selected swing must represent the current approach into the applicable
zone and must not have been superseded; do not search backwards for a convenient
lower level when the current swing fails. Exact corrective-leg identification
is a machine interpretation the charter leaves unspecified. Preserve the
existing causal trigger boundary; never resurrect a trigger that occurred
before the necessary setup evidence was available. No pivot-parameter change
or same-bar pivot confirmation is needed for this correction.

2. Failed alternate entry does not inherently invalidate the deep-add plan

SOURCE: 7.2, line 453: "4H close above the confirm level, then entry on the
first retest within three 4H bars; otherwise skip." The short mirror is line
454. An ordinary out-of-band close means "no ticket" (lines 450/454).
7.10 condition 1 (line 527) explicitly joins ordinary zone triggers OR breakout/
breakdown retests. 7.1 places Type 2/2S invalidation at W2 (438/443), and 7.3
judges invalidation on a daily close (457). 7.11 ties plan direction/status to
the structural verdict (540-548), not to the first missed entry attempt.

RECOMMENDED INTERPRETATION: skip/expire the breakout attempt, while the still
valid deep-add setup remains eligible for a later independent 7.2 trigger.
This follows the entry/setup distinction; the source does not explicitly
specify a software retry state machine.

MINIMAL CORRECTION: RETEST_EXPIRED and FAILED_FIRST_RETEST close only the
alternate branch. TRIGGER_OUTSIDE_ZONE rejects that proposed trigger/ticket;
it is not alone proof of structural invalidation. Keep an otherwise valid
setup WAIT and evaluate future fresh ordinary triggers. Preserve the failed
attempt's evidence and never extend/restart its three-bar window or reuse its
old trigger. Do not revive filled/consumed opportunities. Genuine structure,
stop/target and existing bounded-plan-lifetime terminal rules still apply.
For minimal scope, leave broader post-READY expiry/chase behavior unchanged.
Unlimited breakout retries, relaxed retest tolerance, or ignoring the first
failed retest are separate policy decisions, unnecessary for this correction.

3. Daily W1 breakout followed by a NEW 4H W1 cross is an added double wait

SOURCE: 7.1 calls Type 2 confirmation the W1 break (438/443). 7.2 specifies a
4H close and first retest (453/454), not a prerequisite daily close followed
by another cross. 7.11 requires a decisive W1 break as structural evidence
(536); its "When" item concerns count/CCR updates (548), not a second entry
timer. The DAILY-close condition belongs to radar stage classification in
11.8 (718-724). A radar hit is not itself an entry authorization.

Thus ENGINE_RULES lines 84-86 plus 138-142 combine screening and execution
rules into a stricter policy. A separate revision can arm a causally known
setup before its first eligible 4H W1 break and start the three-bar clock at
that break. Do not backfill an entry from before setup availability. The
charter leaves the move-off-W2 endpoint/freeze unspecified: changing daily
plan creation or freezing it on a 4H bar changes zones and evidence IDs and
needs an explicit machine-policy definition and separate causal replay. It
is not required for the two minimal corrections above. Removing W1 confirmation
altogether is not source-backed.

4. Preserve the risk hurdle; test whether deeper entries become available

7.3 (457-459) requires buffered hard-stop risk at ACTUAL entry: Tier 1 T1 >=
1.5R AND 50/50 blend >=2.5R; Tier 2 >=1.8R AND >=3R, including rounded splits.
7.1 fixes Type 2 targets at W2 +/- 1.0 and 1.618 times W1 length (438/443).
The reported ETH/SOL retests already fail gross R:R (ENGINE_RULES 291-307).
Correct timing can restore access to deeper pullbacks/higher short bounces;
it cannot make those same bad-price entries pass. Lower hurdles, tighter
nonstructural stops, larger targets or unconfirmed pivots would be deliberate
policy revisions, not corrections justified by these clauses.
